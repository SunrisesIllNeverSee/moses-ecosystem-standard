# MO§ES Governance Architecture

> **Handbook ID**: MOS-HB-GOV-001
> **Authority sources**: MO-DEF-001, MO-PROBLEM-001
> **Status**: published

## What it is

MO§ES™ is the enforcement architecture that operationalizes the Conservation
Law of Commitment. It preserves commitment content through transformation via
compression gating, lineage tracking, and hardware anchoring.

## The problem it addresses

When an AI operator transforms a signal (summarizes, translates, compresses,
restructures), the commitment content — the obligations, prohibitions, and
modal constraints — can degrade. Without governance, this degradation is
irreversible.

MO§ES addresses this at the execution layer: it governs the transformation
itself, ensuring that commitment is conserved through the process.

## How it works (public layer)

- **Compression gating**: transformations pass through an enforcement gate
- **Lineage tracking**: every transformation is traced
- **Hardware anchoring**: commitments are anchored to physical substrate

The proprietary implementation details are not public. Patent Serial No.
63/877,177 (Provisional) covers the operational system.
